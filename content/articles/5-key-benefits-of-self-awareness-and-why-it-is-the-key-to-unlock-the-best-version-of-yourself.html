Five key benefits of being Self-aware to unlock the best version of yourself

1. Improved Decision Making

When you have an accurate understanding of yourself, you can make better decisions because you know what strategies work best for you and which ones don’t. You won’t waste time on ideas or approaches that aren’t a good fit for your personality or values. As a result, decision-making becomes more efficient and effective, leading to better outcomes.

I have seen on many occasions as employees progress in the organizations they become very good at following directions and fulfilling tight deadlines but over time they lose their identity and struggle to express opinions or make decisions at key moments.

Understanding your own values, and drivers will not only help you to make better decisions but will also establish your core identity.

2. Increased Confidence

The more you understand yourself—your strengths, weaknesses, and motivations—the more confidence you gain in your ability to lead. A lack of self-awareness can lead to feelings of insecurity or doubt as you try to navigate unfamiliar situations or challenges as they arise. By developing self-awareness, however, you can build their confidence so you feel secure in your role as a leader no matter what comes in your way.

At the organizational level, there are processes such as 360 feedback, ongoing feedback from line managers, and other training. Unfortunately on many occasions, these are taken as formalities meaning you fill up a form, have a conversation, and make a plan on paper.

How many of you have genuinely tried to work on yourself? If the answer is ‘Yes’ then big Congratulations, if the answer is ‘To some extent’ or ‘No’ then it’s high time you start working on it.

3. Increased Resilience

Self-aware individuals are typically more resilient when it comes to stressful or challenging situations because they recognize how their emotions may be impacting their reactions and can use this knowledge to adapt accordingly in order to remain calm under pressure instead of letting negative thoughts control their actions or words in difficult moments. This increased resilience makes them even more effective as leaders because they know how best to handle delicate situations while still remaining strong enough to lead by example during times of turmoil or crisis within an organization or team environment. How resilient are you? When was the last time you successfully bounced back from a setback?

Could you have done something differently?

Again it all starts with self-awareness.

4. Improved Leadership Skills

Self-awareness enables you to recognize when you need help from others or when it’s best to delegate tasks to those who are better equipped for certain roles than yourself—allowing employees to shine in areas where they excel instead of trying to do everything by yourself (which often leads to burnout). Plus, it helps you to become more open-minded when making decisions so that you can consider all perspectives before coming to any conclusions. I am sure you have had the chance to work with leaders who are empowering, collaborative, and open-minded but you must have also come across leaders who are not clear in their brief, tends to keep things to themselves (maybe slightly insecure), and ultimately hinders team development. What do you think makes the difference? Again it all starts with a deep level of Self-awareness.

5. Improved Productivity

When you are more aware of their emotions and reactions, you are better able to focus on the tasks at hand rather than getting caught up in your own feelings or thoughts. This allows you to stay productive and focused on the goals you need to accomplish in all aspects of your life. It also helps you avoid procrastination and other distractions that can impact your productivity levels over time.
