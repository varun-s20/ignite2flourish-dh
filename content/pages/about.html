I have worked in local, regional and global roles in many countries, including Australia, the UK, Hong Kong, New Zealand, Indonesia, Bangladesh and Pakistan. I have worked as an HR director in different markets and have reported directly to management board members at group level.

Along the way I have had the privilege of working with colleagues from more than 100 countries, and of mentoring professionals from the USA, the UK, Brazil, Australia, Bangladesh, Indonesia, India, the UAE, Pakistan, New Zealand, Hong Kong, Chile, Malaysia and Sri Lanka, among others. Those experiences have made me confident yet humble, and very open-minded.

I have had plenty of success and plenty of learning, and I have gone through ups and downs, professionally and personally. I have come out of them more grounded. I am still learning. I am married, with two boys.

After years of conversations with people who were capable but stuck, or simply going with the flow, I wanted a place to share what I have learned and to work through the problems professionals meet in their working lives.

Ignite2Flourish is that place. It connects best practice and research with personal experience: articles on work, leadership and careers; reviews of the books and research papers worth your time; and ideas from mindset and positive psychology that help people move from languishing to flourishing.
