I’ve worked in local, regional and global HR roles across Australia, the UK, Hong Kong, New Zealand, Indonesia, Bangladesh and Pakistan. For more than 15 years I’ve been part of the executive team in different organisations, as an HR director in different markets, reporting directly to management board members at group level.

Along the way I’ve worked with colleagues from more than 100 countries, and mentored professionals from more than a dozen, from the USA and Brazil to the UAE, India and Sri Lanka. It has taught me to be confident in my views and open to being wrong.

There’s been success, and plenty of setbacks, professionally and personally. They’ve left me more grounded, and still learning. Outside work, I’m married with two boys.

For years I’ve had conversations with capable people who felt stuck, or who were simply going with the flow. I wanted a place to share what I’ve learned and to work through the problems professionals face at work.

That place is Ignite2Flourish. It connects research and best practice with what I’ve seen first-hand: essays on work, leadership and careers; reviews of books and research papers worth your time; and ideas from positive psychology, tested rather than taken on trust.
